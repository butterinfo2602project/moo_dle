How To Play

Try to guess the hidden 4-digit code.

  • Enter 4 different digits to make your guess.
  • You cannot repeat a digit in the same guess.
  • You only get one game per day.
Example
Actual number:
5
1
3
4
Your guess:
5
2
6
3

This would give you 1 Bull and 1 Cow.
Bull = right digit in the right spot.
Cow = right digit in the wrong spot.
